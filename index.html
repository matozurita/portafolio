CanadaGOGo Into The WorldCheck-in de VidaEdu Experts

The challenge

Study-abroad programs cost $2,000–$5,000. In a feed full of identical offers, a post has about a second to make someone stop and picture themselves there.

What I did

I designed promo posts and multi-slide carousels for Spanish-speaking audiences. Each one has one hook, one number and one call to action, and they stay consistent with each agency's brand.

The craft

I tie hooks to the moment, like a football red card, a summer in New York or a winter intake. I use price anchors and lead with data. Carousels end in a comment-keyword CTA so leads can be handled automatically.

The pattern behind every post

  1. Hook tied to the moment or a feeling, never a generic “Study abroad!”.
  2. One number big enough to read on a phone at a glance.
  3. One action: comment a keyword, send a DM, or save the post.
  4. Brand first. Each agency keeps its own colours and type, even when the same person designs for all of them.

JC Ecuador
Rides

Brand identityBilingual EN/ESWebsiteTracking

The brief

A family business: private day trips and airport transfers from Quito, run by a licensed tourism guide. It had no brand, no website and no system, only WhatsApp.

What I built

I built the whole thing myself: visual identity, a bilingual website, print pieces, Instagram content, a Google Business Profile, and a GA4, Meta Pixel and Clarity setup that only loads after cookie consent.

The strategic call

I found that transport and tour sales need a licence the business doesn't have yet. So I moved the content toward local-guide tips to grow an audience now, instead of pushing sales it can't legally make yet.
